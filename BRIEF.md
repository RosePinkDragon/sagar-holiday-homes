## 1. Positioning

**What this property actually is:** a private 3BHK villa with a pool and a large open ground, set in a working coconut-and-mango orchard, five minutes' drive from Saldure beach.

**What it is not:** a sea-view property. Do not compete on the beach. Dapoli's beachfront inventory is large and better positioned for that claim.

**The three real differentiators, in order:**

1. **Space.** ~150m of open ground for cricket, football, badminton. Most Konkan villas sit on small plots. This is rare and it is the lead.
2. **Private pool under a gazebo.** Shaded, fenced enclosure, whole-villa exclusive — not shared with other guests.
3. **Working orchard.** ~30 coconut and mango trees. Alphonso country. April–May mango season is a marketable event, not filler.

Supporting: full guest-usable kitchen plus an on-call local cook — groups can self-cater or order. Resorts nearby cannot offer this.

**Positioning statement (draft):**

> A private villa in a Konkan orchard — the whole house, the whole pool, and a ground big enough for a real cricket match. Beach five minutes away.

**Target guest segments:**

- Extended families (multi-generational, 8–12 people)
- Friend groups from Mumbai and Pune
- Small corporate offsites (parking supports this; see §3)

**Competitive note:** the nearest incumbent, Salt Water Beach Resort, is room-wise and rated ~3.9–4.1, with recurring public complaints about unresponsive service. A staffed phone line 10am–10pm is a genuine differentiator. Say so on the site.

## 3. Pool — safety and disclosure

The enclosure is fenced with controlled access, which addresses the main risk (unsupervised entry). Two things remain:

**6ft uniform depth means no child or non-swimmer can stand up anywhere in it.** This is a supervised-only pool. Disclose it plainly — losing a few bookings is cheaper than a preventable incident.

## 4. Rate card **[TBD — owner sign-off required]**

Benchmarked against Dapoli villas with pools (market band roughly ₹9,775–₹17,000/night; comparable 4BHK-with-pool properties sell direct from ₹25,000). Adjusted down for 3BHK and no sea view.

| Period            | Rate/night        |
| ----------------- | ----------------- |
| Weekday (Mon–Thu) | ₹10,000 – ₹12,000 |
| Weekend (Fri–Sun) | ₹15,000 – ₹18,000 |
| Peak              | ₹22,000 – ₹26,000 |

**Peak dates:** Diwali · 25 Dec – 2 Jan · Holi · all long weekends · May summer holidays

- Base occupancy **8**. Extra guest ₹800–1,000/night, up to 12.
- **Launch offer [ASSUMED]:** 20–25% off published rates for the first 3 months, in exchange for a Google review. Discount _off_ a published rate — never lower the base rate itself. Anchoring low is hard to undo.

### The GST decision — blocks all content **[TBD]**

Accommodation above ₹7,500/night attracts **18% GST with input tax credit** (below that it's 5% without ITC). A whole-villa buyout is a single unit of accommodation, so every realistic rate here sits above the line — meaning 18%, but the build-out, furnishings and running costs become creditable.

**Decide before any copy is written: are displayed rates GST-inclusive or exclusive?** Inclusive converts better for leisure guests — nobody enjoys an 18% surprise at checkout — but it changes every number on the site and on every OTA listing.

Confirm the treatment with a CA. Ask specifically about:

- TCS collected by OTAs under the e-commerce operator provisions (appears in GSTR-8 data; must be reconciled and claimed)
- Reverse charge on commission billed by foreign OTA entities
- Credit-note treatment for refunds against issued invoices

Worth checking whether the owner's CA has hospitality clients. If not, find one who does.

## 5. Booking & cancellation policy

**Confirmation**

- 30% advance to confirm. Balance at check-in.
- Booking confirmed only on receipt of advance. Dates held 24 hours pending payment.

**Cancellation — refund of the advance**

| Notice before check-in | Refund                       |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 15+ days               | Full, less ₹2,000 processing |
| 7–14 days              | 50%                          |
| 3–6 days               | 25%                          |
| Under 3 days / no-show | None                         |

**Date transfer — offer before refund.** One free date change if requested 7+ days out, valid 90 days, subject to availability, rate difference payable. This clause protects the calendar while keeping the policy from reading as harsh. Most guests take it.

**Stay rules**

- Check-in 1pm · Check-out 11am
- Free early check-in (11am) and late check-out (2pm) when the adjacent night is unbooked, on request. Costs nothing on an empty calendar and materially improves the 1-night experience given the 5-hour drive.
- **Minimum stay:** 1 night by default. 2 nights on peak dates only.
- **Single-night Saturday: +25%.** A Saturday-only booking kills the Friday and the Sunday. Most guests add the night instead.

**Other terms**

- Refundable security deposit ₹5,000 **[ASSUMED]**, returned within 48 hours of check-out
- Guests exceeding the declared count may be refused entry or charged double the extra-guest rate
- Music off by 10pm — noise complaints in a small village become real problems
- Pool disclosure per §3
- **Force majeure:** full date transfer for landslides, cyclones or road closures. Konkan monsoon makes this necessary, not optional.

> OTA cancellation policies are set inside their own systems and won't match this exactly. On Airbnb, choose the **Firm** or **Strict** preset.

## 7. Operations model

**Do not hire a dedicated enquiry person.** At 5–8 bookings/month (~30–60 enquiries) that's 30–45 minutes of work a day — a full-time salary for a part-time task, and a straight loss in slow months.

**Instead:** fold enquiry handling into the on-site caretaker/manager role, which is needed anyway for keys, check-in, cleaning and coordinating the cook. Provide a WhatsApp Business number, the rate card, calendar access, and a per-booking incentive of ₹300–500 on confirmed direct bookings. Cost becomes variable and scales with revenue. Owner handles escalations.

**Response time is a ranking factor** on Airbnb and Booking.com, and a slow reply loses peak-date bookings outright. If no named person exists by launch week, delay the OTA listings — not the website.

### Channel strategy

Launch on **two channels only: own site + Airbnb.** Run one shared Google Calendar as the single source of truth, updated the moment anything is confirmed.

**Double-booking is the critical failure mode.** With one sellable unit there is no second room to move a group of 12 into — a clash means cancelling a family that drove five hours, an OTA penalty, and a permanent review. At two channels and ~6 bookings/month, a disciplined calendar is sufficient.

**Trigger to buy a channel manager:** three or more OTAs, or more than 10 bookings/month. Do not wait for the first clash. Budget ₹1,500–3,000/month (eZee, Djubo Express, Stayflexi).

> Direct API access to Airbnb/Booking.com is not available to individual hosts — it's restricted to certified partners. If a custom admin panel is built later, it integrates against the **channel manager's** API, not the OTAs' own.

### Books

**Zoho Books free tier.** Genuinely free with no time limit under ₹25 lakh turnover, covering 1 user + 1 accountant and 1,000 transactions/year. At ~60–100 invoices annually this is comfortably sufficient. Upgrade to Standard (₹899 + GST) only on crossing the threshold.

**Recurring costs go on the owner's card, in his name and email, from day one** — domain, hosting, gateway, channel manager. Otherwise the developer becomes the permanent billing department.

**Reconciliation warning:** money arrives three ways and the bank statement will never match revenue. Direct (you collect, you invoice), Booking.com (you collect at property, they invoice you commission separately), Airbnb (they collect, pay out net of commission after check-in).

**Estimated fixed monthly cost at launch: under ₹1,000** (domain + hosting). Payment gateway ~2% of direct revenue only.

## 8. Website — job and structure

**Primary job:** capture enquiries and build enough trust for a ₹15,000+ commitment.
**Secondary:** be the property's credible presence when someone Googles the name after seeing an OTA listing.
**Not in v1:** online payment. Add it once the rate card, policy and traffic justify escaping OTA commission.

### Site map

| Page             | Route               | Job                                               |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Home             | `/`                 | Positioning, hero image, key facts, enquiry CTA   |
| The Villa        | `/villa`            | Rooms, occupancy, amenities, floor logic          |
| Pool & Grounds   | `/pool-and-grounds` | Pool, gazebo, ground, orchard + safety disclosure |
| Gallery          | `/gallery`          | Photo grid, categorised                           |
| Food             | `/food`             | Kitchen, cook, sample menu, pricing               |
| Location         | `/location`         | Map, drive times, beaches, things to do nearby    |
| Tariff & Booking | `/tariff`           | Rate card, policy, what's included                |
| Contact          | `/contact`          | Enquiry form, phone, WhatsApp, address            |

One page per search intent. A single-page site cannot rank for "villa with pool Dapoli" _and_ "Dapoli villa with kitchen" — separate pages can.

### Page content requirements

**Home**

- Hero: pool with orchard behind it. This is the single most important image on the site.
- H1 containing "Dapoli" and "private pool"
- Three-fact strip: sleeps 12 · private pool · 150m open ground
- Positioning paragraph, the three differentiators, 4–6 photo teaser, testimonials block (empty at launch — design for it now), sticky enquiry CTA
- Never state a rate on the home page without stating whether it includes GST

**Pool & Grounds**

- Pool photos including one wide shot showing the gazebo and fencing
- Dimensions and depth stated plainly
- Safety disclosure verbatim from §3
- Ground: what can actually be played there
- Orchard, with a seasonal note on mango season

**Location**

- Embedded map pin at the actual gate, not the village centre
- Drive times from Mumbai and Pune with route note
- Nearby: Saldure beach (5 min), Murud beach, Harnai fish market, Kelshi, Suvarnadurg fort
- This page is where local SEO is won — name the landmarks people search

**Tariff & Booking**

- Full rate card, GST treatment stated explicitly
- Complete cancellation policy
- What's included vs extra
- Peak-date calendar

### Enquiry form fields

Name · Phone (required, primary contact channel) · Email · Check-in date · Check-out date · Number of guests · Meals needed (yes/no/tell me more) · Message

- Keep it to one screen. Every additional field costs conversions.
- Phone is required; email is not. Indian leisure bookings close on the phone.
- **Pair every form with a WhatsApp deep link** (`wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=...` prefilled with the property name). This will out-convert the form.
- Confirmation must state the callback window explicitly: "We'll call you back between 10am and 10pm."

### SEO requirements

- `LodgingBusiness` JSON-LD: name, address, geo, amenities, `priceRange`, `numberOfRooms`, photos
- Unique title + meta description per page. Target: "villa with private pool in Dapoli", "3BHK villa Dapoli", "villa near Saldure beach", "Dapoli villa for family groups"
- OpenGraph images per page — WhatsApp link sharing is the primary distribution channel in this market
- `sitemap.xml` + `robots.txt`
- All images AVIF/WebP, lazy-loaded below fold, explicit dimensions to avoid layout shift
- Descriptive alt text on every image
- **Zero duplicate copy from competitor sites.** Everything written fresh.
- Mobile-first: the majority of traffic will be phones on mid-range Android

### Editability

Rates, blocked dates, policy text, amenities and photos will change without the developer. Keep all of it in a single typed content module (`content/property.ts`), never hardcoded into components. Swap that one file for a CMS in phase 2.

## 12. Scope note (developer ↔ owner)

Worth putting in writing, even informally on WhatsApp. Family projects fail on unstated assumptions far more often than on technical problems.

**In scope:** website design and build; Google Business Profile setup; OTA listing setup (per platform).
**Not in scope:** enquiry handling; ongoing content updates; day-to-day OTA management; rate changes after launch.
**Owner provides:** photos, rates, policy sign-off, all TBD items above — by an agreed date.
**Bug fixes:** free for 90 days where a delivered feature doesn't work as specified. Afterwards still free, but on the developer's timeline.
**Changes** (new pages, new features, new requirements) are quoted separately. Every time.
**Recurring costs** are on the owner's accounts from day one.

_If the property slips, the website timeline slips with it — agree this now rather than during launch week._
